## Meet Destiny: Your New AI Buddy
- Say hello to Destiny, a built-in smart assistant designed to help you tackle your daily tasks with ease.
- Ask questions naturally just like you would to a friend, completely skipping the need for awkward keyword guessing.
- Switch seamlessly between English and Bulgarian with a single click.
## Powerful Core Tools
- Trigger a superpowered web search by choosing a lightning-fast surface scan, or launch a massive deep dive when you need full detective mode.
- Upload long documents and let the AI extract the most important details, instantly finding specific facts hidden deep inside the text.
- Manage your Jira tasks and Confluence pages without jumping between tabs, keeping your favorite connected tools organized in one place.
## Design and Privacy
- Enjoy a smooth, responsive layout across desktop and tablet devices, featuring both light and dark modes.
- Work against a night-sky background complete with shooting stars, guided by quick pop-up messages and helpful suggestion buttons.
- Rest easy with locked-down account security, giving you full control to save, archive, delete, or download a personal copy of your past conversations.
## Getting Started
- Celebrate our very first public launch by logging into your account, or try out a limited guest mode just to look around.
- Head over to your settings to connect your work accounts, then pick a starting topic or simply say hello to the chat.
- Help us level up by dropping us a line with your brilliant ideas, feature suggestions, or just say hi.
